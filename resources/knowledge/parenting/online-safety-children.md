- Under about two years: little or no screen time apart from video calls; talking, playing and reading matter more.
- Young children: short, chosen programmes, ideally watched together, and no screens during meals or in the hour before sleep.
- Older children and teens: agree rules together (time limits, phone-free bedrooms at night, homework first), and model good habits yourself.
- Use parental controls and safe search, keep devices in shared spaces for younger children, and check apps' age limits (many social media apps are for 13 and over).
- Teach: never share address, school, or photos with strangers; tell a trusted adult if anyone asks for secrets, photos, money, or to meet; blocking and reporting are always OK.
- Grooming signs: an adult or older youth giving gifts or airtime, asking for secrecy, or moving chat to private apps. This is a safeguarding concern; follow the safety rules.
- If a child sees pornography or upsetting content: stay calm, do not punish them for telling, explain in age-appropriate words, and adjust settings.
