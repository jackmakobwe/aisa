- Show interest: ask what they learned today, look at exercise books together, and praise effort, not only marks.
- Routine: a regular quiet time and place for homework, after a snack and rest; short breaks for young children.
- Reading: read together daily, even for 10 minutes, in any language; let them choose stories; ask questions about the story. Parents who cannot read well can still ask the child to read to them and tell the story.
- Struggling child: talk with the teacher early; check sight and hearing at a health facility; break tasks into small steps; avoid harsh punishment or comparison, which reduce motivation.
- Refusing school: find out why (bullying, fear of a teacher, falling behind, illness, abuse). Listen calmly and work with the school.
- Young mothers and pregnant girls: they may continue their education; ask the school or district education office about returning to school.
- School costs: plan a school fund in the family budget, saving a little each month before term starts.
