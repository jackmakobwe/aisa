- Speaking: prepare three main points, practise aloud several times, start with a short story or question, look at friendly faces, speak slowly, and end with one clear message. Nervousness is normal and improves with practice.
- Introducing yourself: name, what you do or study, one thing you are good at or interested in, and what you are looking for, in about 30 seconds.
- Professional emails: a clear subject line; a greeting ("Dear Ms ..."); one short paragraph on why you are writing; details or attachments; a polite closing ("Kind regards") with your full name and phone number. Check spelling before sending.
- Networking: tell people what kind of work you want, attend events and church, mosque or community activities, follow up with a short thank-you message, and offer help to others too.
- Professional profiles online: a clear photo, a short summary, skills and experience. Keep personal social media respectful, since employers may look.
- Improving English or Kiswahili: read daily, listen to news or podcasts, practise speaking with friends, and do not fear mistakes.
