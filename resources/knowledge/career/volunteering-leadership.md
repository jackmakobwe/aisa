- Experience does not only come from paid jobs: volunteering, student leadership, faith or youth group roles, helping in a family business, tutoring, and organising events all count on a CV.
- Choose volunteering that builds skills you want (for example communication, teaching, accounting, digital media) and ask for a reference letter when you finish.
- Leadership grows by taking small responsibilities: organising a meeting, managing a group's money records, or leading a project, then reflecting on what worked.
- Part-time or holiday work: be cautious of offers needing payment upfront, and agree hours and pay before starting.
- Mentors: ask someone whose path you admire for a short conversation; prepare specific questions; respect their time; follow up with thanks.
- Unpaid internships can open doors, but should have a clear learning plan and fixed duration; long unpaid work with no learning is exploitation.
