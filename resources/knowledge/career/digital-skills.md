Building skills
- Start with basics that most jobs use: email, typing, word processing, spreadsheets, safe internet use, and using a smartphone for work (mobile money records, business social media pages, maps).
- Many free courses exist online and at community centres or libraries. Short, practical projects (a CV in a word processor, a simple budget in a spreadsheet, a business page) build confidence and a portfolio.
- AI tools can help draft text, translate, summarise, brainstorm business ideas or practise interview questions. Always check their output, never paste private personal or financial details into them, and use them to learn, not to cheat at school.
- Paths with growing demand include digital marketing, graphic design, data entry and analysis, customer support, coding and phone/computer repair. They take months of practice; be honest that results are not instant.

Earning online: be careful
- Real online work pays you; you should not pay to get it. Be wary of "earn TSh X daily by liking videos", task apps that require deposits, and anything that needs recruiting friends.
- Protect accounts with strong passwords and two-step verification, and never share codes sent by SMS.
- Online safety: think before sharing photos or personal information. If anyone threatens to share private images unless paid or sent more (sextortion), do not pay or send more, keep evidence, block, and tell a trusted person; this is abuse, follow the safety rules.
