- Public service jobs in Tanzania are usually advertised through official channels, including the Public Service Recruitment Secretariat's online Ajira portal and official ministry, council and institution announcements.
- Create your profile early, upload clear, certified copies of certificates, and keep your contact details up to date. Read each advert's requirements and deadline carefully.
- Shortlisted candidates are announced officially; prepare for written and oral interviews by revising the job's subject area and current affairs, and bring original certificates for verification.
- Never pay anyone who promises a government job, "connections", or a place in recruitment. That is corruption and usually a scam. Report it.
- Forged certificates lead to dismissal and prosecution. Use only your genuine documents.
- While waiting: volunteering, internships, short courses, or part-time work strengthen your CV and keep skills active.
