- Write everything down: a simple daily list with the three most important tasks first.
- Break big tasks into small first steps ("open the document and write the title") to beat procrastination.
- Work in focused blocks (25–45 minutes) with short breaks; put the phone face down or on silent during focus time.
- Plan the week on one page: fixed commitments first (work, classes, family duties), then study or business time, then rest.
- Use your best energy hours for the hardest work.
- Learn to say no or "not now" to requests that do not fit your priorities, politely.
- Review each evening: what got done, what moves to tomorrow. Celebrate progress.
- Constant tiredness, low mood or inability to concentrate for weeks may be more than poor planning; encourage rest and seeing a health worker if it continues.
