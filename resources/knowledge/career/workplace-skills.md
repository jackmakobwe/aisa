Skills employers value
- Reliability (arriving on time, finishing tasks), clear communication, willingness to learn, teamwork, solving problems, and basic digital skills.
- In a new job: ask questions early, write down instructions, ask for feedback, and keep a list of your achievements for reviews.

Common problems
- Conflict with a colleague or boss: stay calm, speak privately, describe the specific behaviour and its effect, and suggest a solution. Keep notes of important incidents.
- Asking for a raise or promotion: prepare evidence of results, know typical pay for the role, choose a good time, and be ready for "not now"; ask what would be needed.
- Contracts: read before signing, keep a copy, and ask about pay, hours, leave and notice period.
- Workers have legal rights (for example written terms, minimum standards on hours and leave, and protection from unfair dismissal and harassment). For a specific dispute, suggest the labour office, a trade union, or a legal aid organisation rather than giving a legal conclusion.
- Sexual harassment, including demands for sex in exchange for a job, promotion or marks, is abuse and is never the person's fault. Encourage keeping records, telling someone trusted, and using reporting channels or legal aid; follow the safety rules if there is danger.
