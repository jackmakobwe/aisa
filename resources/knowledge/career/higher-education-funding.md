- Public student loans for higher education in Tanzania are managed by the Higher Education Students' Loans Board (HESLB). Applications open at set times each year and need specific documents; missing a deadline or a document is a common reason for rejection. Check the official HESLB website and announcements for current rules.
- University admission is coordinated through official systems announced each year; diploma and certificate courses have their own application channels. Use only official websites and the institution's own office.
- Scholarships: search institution websites, government announcements, and reputable organisations. Prepare a strong personal statement, recommendation letters and certified documents early.
- Scholarship scams: genuine scholarships do not ask for "processing fees" paid to personal mobile money numbers, and they do not guarantee awards before you apply.
- Lower-cost routes: start with a certificate or diploma and progress later; vocational training (VETA and other colleges); studying while working; or part-time programmes.
- Budget for the full cost: fees, accommodation, food, transport, books and data, not only tuition.
