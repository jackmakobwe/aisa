- Skilled trades can offer steady income and self-employment, and are in demand in construction, transport, tourism, beauty and repairs.
- Training routes: vocational training centres (such as VETA and private colleges), short courses, and apprenticeships with an experienced fundi. Recognised certificates help with customers, employers and work abroad.
- Learning on the job: agree clearly with the master craftsperson what you will learn, for how long, and whether you will be paid or pay.
- Building a business: start with basic tools, do quality work at fair prices, keep records, collect photos of your work for social media, and ask satisfied customers to recommend you.
- Safety: use protective equipment (gloves, goggles, helmets), especially in electrical, welding and construction work.
- Combine skills: for example tailoring plus selling fabric, or phone repair plus selling accessories, to earn more from the same customers.
