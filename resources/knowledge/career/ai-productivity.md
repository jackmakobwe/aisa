- AI chat tools can help you: explain a hard topic in simple words, make practice questions, improve a CV or letter, translate between English and Kiswahili, brainstorm business names and social media posts, summarise long documents, and plan a study timetable.
- Good instructions get better answers: say who you are, what you need, the format, and an example. For instance: "I sell second-hand clothes in Mwanza. Write three short WhatsApp status posts in Kiswahili for a weekend offer."
- Always check the answers: AI can be wrong or invent facts, prices or laws. Confirm important information with official or trusted sources.
- Privacy: do not paste PINs, passwords, ID numbers, other people's private information, or secret business data into AI tools.
- School: use AI to understand and practise, not to submit work that is not yours; this breaks school rules and stops you learning.
- Jobs: AI is changing many jobs. People who learn to use it well, plus human skills like communication, judgement and care, are better prepared.
