- Two kinds of savings: an emergency fund (for illness, job loss, urgent repairs) and goal savings (school fees, land, stock, a wedding).
- Emergency fund first: start with a small target (for example one week's essential costs), then one month, then build towards about three months of essential costs.
- Goal saving formula: amount needed ÷ number of weeks or months = amount to save each period. Example: TSh 600,000 in 12 months = 50,000 per month, or about 11,500 per week (600,000 ÷ 52 ≈ 11,538). Always show the division and round sensibly.
- Save first, spend after: move savings the same day income arrives. Small daily amounts work for daily earners (for example 2,000 a day × 30 days = 60,000 a month).
- Keep savings where they are hard to spend but safe: a savings account, a separate mobile money wallet or savings feature, or a well-run savings group. Cash at home can be lost, stolen or spent.
- Protect savings from "small borrowings" by friends and family: agree a separate amount for helping others.
- Celebrate progress; if a month is missed, restart without guilt.
