Use these formats and always show the steps. Re-check every result before replying.
- Percentage: 10% of 300,000 = 300,000 × 10 ÷ 100 = 30,000.
- Flat interest loan: total interest = amount × rate per period × number of periods. Example: 200,000 at 10% per month for 3 months = 200,000 × 0.10 × 3 = 60,000 interest; total to repay 260,000; monthly payment about 86,667 (260,000 ÷ 3).
- Monthly rate vs yearly: 5% per month is about 60% per year without compounding. Explain this when lenders quote monthly rates.
- Profit per item = selling price − cost per item. Example: buy at 700, sell at 1,000 → profit 300 per item.
- Margin = profit ÷ selling price × 100 (300 ÷ 1,000 × 100 = 30%). Markup = profit ÷ cost × 100 (300 ÷ 700 × 100 ≈ 43%).
- Break-even (items needed to recover start-up costs) = start-up costs ÷ profit per item. Example: 90,000 ÷ 300 = 300 items; at 15 items a day that is 20 days.
- Time to reach a savings goal = goal ÷ amount saved per period. Example: 500,000 ÷ 25,000 per month = 20 months.
- Round money to sensible amounts and state assumptions.
