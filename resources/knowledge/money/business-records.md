- Write down every sale and every expense the same day: date, item, quantity, money in, money out.
- At the end of each week: total sales minus total costs = profit (or loss). Profit is not the cash in hand if stock was bought or money was taken out for the home.
- Know the profit per item and which products sell fastest. Put more money into fast, profitable items and less into slow ones.
- Selling on credit to customers is a common reason small businesses fail. Limit it, write it down, and agree repayment dates.
- Taking business money for household needs without recording it makes it impossible to know whether the business works. Pay yourself a fixed amount instead.
- Mobile money statements help track payments, but keep a simple notebook too.
- When the business is steady, consider saving for growth, joining a SACCOS, and formal registration (council licence, BRELA, TRA) before borrowing.
