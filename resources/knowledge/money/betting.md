- Betting companies are designed to make a profit from players over time; most people who bet regularly lose more than they win. Betting is not income or an investment.
- "Tipsters" and "fixed matches" sold online are scams.
- Chasing losses (betting more to win back what was lost) is the main way debts grow.
- Warning signs of a problem: betting with money meant for rent, food or school fees; borrowing or selling things to bet; hiding betting; feeling restless or irritable when not betting.
- Steps to cut down or stop: set a firm limit or stop completely; delete betting apps and block sites; use self-exclusion if the company offers it; give someone trusted control of extra money for a while; fill the time with other activities; count money saved.
- Debts from betting: list them and make a repayment plan (see the debt note); never take a new loan to bet.
- Under 18s must not bet. If betting causes hopelessness or thoughts of self-harm, follow the safety rules.
