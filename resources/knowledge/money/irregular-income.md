Many people (traders, boda boda riders, farmers, casual workers, freelancers) earn different amounts each day or season.

Approach
- Find the "base month": the essential costs the person must cover every month. Plan around their lowest normal month, not their best.
- Use a "salary pot": put all earnings in one place (for example a separate mobile money wallet or savings account) and pay yourself a fixed weekly or monthly amount from it. Good months top up the pot; poor months draw from it.
- Save a percentage rather than a fixed amount: for example 10% of every payment, on the same day it arrives. Save more on good days.
- Keep business money apart from personal money. For traders, restocking money is not spending money.
- Build a buffer of at least one month of essential costs before taking on new commitments, then grow it.
- Seasonal earners (farmers, tourism workers) can divide the season's income across the months until the next season.
- Savings groups (VICOBA, SACCOS, rotating groups among trusted people) give discipline and access to small loans. Only join groups with clear written rules, records, and members you know.
- Betting and "quick money" schemes are not a way to smooth income. They usually make things worse.
