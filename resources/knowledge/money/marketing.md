- Know your customer: who buys, where they are, what problem you solve for them, and what they can pay.
- Make it easy to buy: a clear price list, good photos, a phone number for orders, convenient delivery or pickup, and mobile money payment.
- Use free tools well: WhatsApp status and a catalogue, a business page on social media, regular posts showing products, customers (with permission) and reviews.
- Quality and trust bring repeat customers: be on time, keep promises, and fix problems politely. Ask happy customers to tell friends.
- Pricing: cover all costs plus profit (see the small business note). Compete on reliability, quality or service rather than always being cheapest.
- Try small promotions with a clear end date (for example a bundle offer), and track whether sales really increased.
- Online selling risks: fake customers asking you to send goods before paying, or "overpayment" tricks. Confirm payment in your own balance before sending goods.
