- Never share your PIN or the confirmation codes sent by SMS, even with someone who says they work for the network, a bank or the government. Real staff never ask for them.
- Cover the keypad when entering a PIN at an agent, and check the amount and name before confirming any payment.
- Messages saying "money was sent to you by mistake, please send it back": check your actual balance or statement first. Most are scams. If a real mistake happened, the provider can reverse it.
- If you send money to the wrong number, contact your provider's customer care immediately with the transaction ID.
- If your SIM suddenly stops working, it could be a SIM swap; contact your provider quickly.
- Keep your phone locked, use different PINs for phone and money, and change PINs if someone may know them.
- Fees: sending, withdrawing and paying bills have charges. Compare costs; sometimes paying directly (merchant payment) is cheaper than withdrawing cash.
- Bank accounts: compare monthly fees and minimum balances; keep statements; a basic or group account can be cheaper.
