- Talk about money openly as a couple: incomes, debts, household costs, support to each family, and shared goals. Agree a household budget and who pays what.
- Many couples use a mix: a shared pot for household costs and goals, and some personal money each partner controls.
- Every adult benefits from some financial independence: their own savings, the ability to earn, and knowing about family assets and debts. This protects the whole family if illness, death or separation happens.
- Keep important documents (land, house, business registration, marriage and birth certificates) safe and known to both partners.
- Taking a partner's income against their will, stopping them from working, or keeping them without money for basic needs is economic abuse; follow the safety rules if there is control or fear.
- Supporting parents and relatives: decide together on a regular, affordable amount; explain your own commitments kindly; offer non-money help when you cannot give cash.
- Property and inheritance questions (land, widows' rights): suggest a legal aid organisation rather than giving legal conclusions.
