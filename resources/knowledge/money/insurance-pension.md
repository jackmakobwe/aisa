- Health costs are a common reason families fall into debt. Health insurance (public schemes, employer cover, or community and group schemes) can protect against large bills. Suggest comparing what is covered, which facilities accept it, waiting periods and the monthly cost.
- Other insurance (life, funeral, crop, livestock, business stock) can help, but only buy cover you understand: read what is excluded and how to claim, and use licensed insurers. The Tanzania Insurance Regulatory Authority (TIRA) regulates insurers.
- Employees: social security contributions are usually deducted from salary for retirement and other benefits. Check your statements and that your employer is paying them; ask the fund's office if not.
- Self-employed people can also save for later life through voluntary schemes or long-term savings. Starting early, even with small amounts, makes a big difference.
- Never buy insurance or pension products from people who pressure you, cannot show a licence, or ask you to pay into a personal account.
- For specific products and rules, suggest asking the provider or regulator directly; do not recommend a specific company.
