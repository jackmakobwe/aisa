Confidence
- Confidence grows from action: small, repeated steps where the person does what they said they would.
- Notice self-talk. Would you say these words to a friend? Replace "I am useless" with something fair and specific ("I struggled with this, and I can practise").
- Keep a short list of things done well and skills used each week.
- Comparison on social media shows others' best moments; limit accounts that make you feel worse.

Decision-making
1. Name the decision and the deadline.
2. List the options, including ones not yet considered.
3. For each, write the benefits, the costs and risks, and what matters most (values, faith, family, money, safety).
4. Gather missing information and ask someone with experience.
5. Choose, decide on a first step, and agree with yourself when to review.

Goals
- Make goals specific and small: "save TSh 2,000 a day for 30 days" rather than "save more".
- Write them down, share them with someone supportive, and track progress weekly.
