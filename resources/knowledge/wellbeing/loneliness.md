- Loneliness is common, especially after moving for work or study, a breakup, becoming a parent, or losing someone. It is not a sign that something is wrong with the person.
- Small, regular contact helps more than one big effort: greeting the same neighbours, a weekly call to a relative, joining one group.
- Groups built around an activity are easier than meeting strangers: faith groups, sports, choirs, savings groups, volunteering, study groups, women's or youth groups.
- Reconnect with one old friend with a simple message.
- Online friends can help, but encourage some face-to-face contact and caution with strangers who ask for money, photos or secrecy.
- Be kind to yourself: rejection or slow progress is normal while building connections.
- If loneliness comes with hopelessness or thoughts of not wanting to live, follow the safety rules.
