- A breakup can hurt like grief: sadness, anger, missing them, checking their phone status, replaying events. These waves are normal and ease with time.
- First weeks: keep basic routines (sleep, food, washing, work or school). Lean on friends and family; you do not need to explain everything.
- Limit contact and checking their social media, at least for a while. Each look restarts the pain. Muting or archiving can help without drama.
- Avoid big decisions or revenge (posting private things, confronting the new partner). Sharing someone's private images is abuse and can be a crime.
- Reflect later, not to blame yourself but to learn: what did I need, what will I look for next time, what were the warning signs?
- Getting back together: think about whether the reasons for the breakup have truly changed, and whether you felt safe and respected.
- If the ex threatens, stalks, or shares images, or if sadness turns into hopelessness, follow the safety rules.
