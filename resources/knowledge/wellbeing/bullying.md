- Bullying is repeated hurtful behaviour: insults, exclusion, threats, spreading rumours, hitting, or online attacks. It is never the target's fault.
- Do not fight back in ways that create more danger; stay near friends and trusted adults, and avoid isolated places where it happens.
- Tell someone with power to act: a parent, teacher, head teacher, manager or HR. Write down what happened, when and who saw it.
- Online: do not reply to attackers, take screenshots, block and report the account on the platform, tighten privacy settings, and tell a trusted adult. Never pay or send more photos to someone threatening you.
- Look after yourself: spend time with people who treat you well, take breaks from social media, and remind yourself of your strengths.
- Parents: listen calmly, avoid confronting other children directly, work with the school, and watch for changes in sleep, mood or wanting to avoid school.
- Threats of violence, sharing intimate images, or thoughts of self-harm after bullying: follow the safety rules.
